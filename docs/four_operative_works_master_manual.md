# SECTION 6: CONTRAINDICATIONS, HAZARDS & EMERGENCY REVERSAL PROTOCOLS

### 6.1 The Mechanics of Psychic Recoil (*Raj'ah*)
In classical Arabic grimoires, psychic backfire is termed *Al-Raj'ah* (الرجعة). It occurs when:
1. **Binding work is cast unjustly against an innocent soul**: The Saturnian weight reflects directly onto the operator's bones, joints, and financial liquidity.
2. **Love attraction magic is attempted while Venus is in detriment/retrograde**: The operator becomes trapped in the very obsessive net intended for the target, while the target feels revulsion.
3. **Protection is cast without prior cleansing**: The ward seals internal miasma and parasites *inside* the energetic vessel, accelerating physical illness or emotional decay.

### 6.2 Emergency Counter-Rites & Unbinding Procedures

#### The Universal Unbinding Protocol (*Fakk al-'Aqd wa-l-Halal*)
If an accidental binding occurs, or a practitioner wishes to cancel a previously installed defixio:
1. **Timing:** Wednesday during the Hour of Mercury or Monday during the Hour of the Moon.
2. **Action:**
   - Retrieve the padlock if possible and unlock it using the key while reciting Psalm 146:7 (*"The Lord looseth the prisoners"*) and Surah Al-Inshirah (94:1-3).
   - If the padlock cannot be retrieved, take 7 keys of cold iron and submerge them in a bowl of boiling water infused with rue, hyssop, and unrefined sea salt.
   - Speak over the steam: *"What was knotted is loosed; what was locked is opened; what was bound in darkness is released in the name of the Living One who never dieth."*
   - Wash your hands and wrists with the cooled water.

#### Neutralizing Psychic Backfire (*Ibtal al-Raj'ah*)
If experiencing acute backlash (sudden dizziness, cold sweats, irrational terror, or electrical appliances burning out):
1. Immediately lie flat on the bare earth or bathroom tile.
2. Place a piece of raw black tourmaline or a heavy piece of iron upon your solar plexus.
3. Drink 1 glass of cold spring water with a pinch of sea salt and lemon juice.
4. Recite three times the Verse of Light (Surah An-Nur 24:35) to realign the auric frequencies.
