- **Meteorological, Climatological, and Geophysical Agency of Indonesia (BMKG):** Official Jakarta astronomical ephemerides, October 2026 sunrise (05:36 WIB) and sunset (17:46–17:47 WIB) tables.
- **Lunar Calendars & Ephemerides:** Astronomy.HK (Third Quarter phase 3 Oct 2026 13:25 UTC).
- **Planetary Event Tables:** Asteria (Sun-Saturn opposition 4 Oct 2026 12:29 UTC / 19:29 WIB).
- **Ephemeris Calendars:** Horoscope Almanac (Venus retrograde station at 8°29′ Scorpio, 3 Oct 2026).
- **Night Sky Visibility:** Time and Date (Jakarta horizon coordinates, planetary culmination and rising times).
