## 2. The 4 Universal Functional Modalities (*Al-Maqāsid al-Arba'ah*)

In *Picatrix* (Books I & IV) and *Shams al-Ma'arif*, any mundane event or ritual operation belongs to one of four elemental directions:

```
                          [ATTRACTION / INCREASE]
                         Jalb wa-Taysir (Prosthesis)
                         Growing, Calling In, Uniting
                                      ▲
                                      │
[BINDING / LOCKING] ◄─────────────────┼─────────────────► [DISSOLUTION / SEVERING]
 'Aqd wa-Tathbit                      │                    Tafriq wa-Ibtal (Lysis)
 Freezing, Securing, Fixing           │                    Cutting, Purging, Banishing
                                      ▼
                           [DEFENSE / SHIELDING]
                         Hifz wa-Tahsin (Apotropaismo)
                         Warding, Guarding, Deflecting
```

1. **Attraction & Cultivation (*Jalb wa-Taysir*):** Drawing in prosperity, love, vitality, benefactors, wisdom, and opportunities.
2. **Protection & Shielding (*Hifz wa-Tahsin*):** Establishing psychic armor, deflecting malice, preserving health, and guarding assets against theft.
3. **Binding & Fixing (*'Aqd wa-Tathbīt*):** Locking boundaries, silencing malicious tongues, halting legal attacks, and stabilizing unstable volatile situations.
4. **Dissolution & Severance (*Tafriq wa-Ibtāl*):** Cord-cutting, breaking curses, dispelling illnesses, dissolving toxic entanglements, and shattering oppressive structures.
